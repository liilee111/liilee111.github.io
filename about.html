About Me

I’m an MSc student in computer science with a background in physics. I’ve studied and worked across the UK, Germany, and Taiwan, moving between academic research and industry roles.

I enjoy physics not just as a subject, but as a way of thinking. It has shaped how I solve problems and design systems in computer science.

My work spans multiple areas, including machine learning, data engineering, software development and scientific computing. I’m particularly interested in numerical simulation in physics and performance optimisation for compute-intensive systems.

My day-to-day workflow is centred on C, Python, Java and JavaScript within Linux-based environments. I’m currently expanding my technical skills in low-level systems programming and high-performance computing to solve complex, large-scale computational problems.
